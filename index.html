Privacy Policy

For the Android games Arrow Path, Tile Match and Bullet Storm (the "Games"), published by YourStudio.
Last updated: 8 November 2026

Summary

The Games do not ask for an account, your name, email address, location, contacts or photos. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. The Games show ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to serve those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The Games save your level progress, scores, records, streaks and settings (such as difficulty, theme, language, sound and vibration) locally on your device. This data is never sent to us. Uninstalling a Game or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Games use Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown or tapped);
  • crash and performance diagnostics related to ads.

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google processes it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch you are asked whether Google may use your data for personalised ads. You can change this at any time in each Game under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Local data: you can erase your progress inside each Game (Settings → Reset) or by uninstalling it.

Permissions

The Games use internet access to load ads, and the advertising ID permission used by Google Mobile Ads. Vibration is used for haptic feedback and can be turned off in Settings. The Games do not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

We do not operate servers that store your data. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date above will be updated.
